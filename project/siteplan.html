N Naija Food Heritage

A celebration of Nigeria's food, people, and traditions

WDD 131 · W05 PROJECT

Website Planning Document

This plan defines the audience, purpose, visual identity, and proposed home-page layout for a website about traditional Nigerian foods.

1. Site Name

N

Naija Food Heritage

Optional domain idea: naijafoodheritage.com

Reason for the name: “Naija” is a familiar informal name for Nigeria, while “Food Heritage” communicates the traditions, stories, and cultural knowledge connected to Nigerian meals. The name is memorable and tells visitors what the website is about.

2. Site Purpose

Naija Food Heritage will introduce visitors to the variety and cultural importance of traditional Nigerian foods. It will provide clear descriptions of popular dishes, their main ingredients, preparation information, and the communities or occasions associated with them.

The completed website is planned to include at least three connected pages:

  • Home: an introduction to Nigerian food culture and featured dishes.
  • Discover Nigerian Dishes: dish profiles, ingredients, origins, and food categories.
  • Share a Food Story: a form where visitors can submit a favourite Nigerian dish or family food tradition.

Interactive features will help visitors explore dishes by category and may remember a visitor's saved favourite using local storage.

3. Scenarios

These questions represent information that a visitor interested in Nigerian cuisine may want to find:

  1. What are the main ingredients used to prepare jollof rice, egusi soup, and moi moi?
  2. Which Nigerian dishes are commonly served at weddings, festivals, and family celebrations?
  3. How can I learn about the cultural background of a traditional dish from a particular part of Nigeria?
  4. Can I share my family's favourite Nigerian recipe or food tradition with the website?

4. Color Schema

The palette uses colours inspired by Nigeria's natural landscape and the warmth of shared meals. The cream background keeps pages comfortable to read, while green and gold provide clear visual emphasis.

Forest green — #174C3C

Used for the header, section headings, navigation, and footer.

Warm gold — #D9A441

Used for buttons, logo accents, borders, and small highlights.

Warm cream — #FFF8E8

Used as the main page background and for light content panels.

Charcoal — #26332E

Used for paragraph text to provide strong contrast on light backgrounds.

5. Typography

Playfair Display — headings

Used for the main page title, section headings, and featured dish names. Its distinctive letterforms give the site a warm editorial character.

Lato — body text

Used for paragraphs, navigation, labels, forms, and captions. It is clear and readable on both mobile and desktop screens.

Layout notes: On mobile, content is arranged in a single column for easy scrolling and touch navigation. On wider screens, the hero uses two columns and featured dishes appear side by side. Images will have meaningful alternative text and use lazy loading where appropriate.

Planned Functionality for W06

  • Responsive navigation shared across all pages.
  • A dish collection generated from JavaScript objects stored in an array, with category filtering.
  • Interactive buttons and form submission with appropriate validation and feedback.
  • A saved-favourite feature using localStorage.
  • JavaScript functions using DOM selection, event listeners, conditional statements, array methods, and template literals.

All externally sourced text and images will be credited on a separate references page linked in the website footer.
