Lagos grew around a network of islands, creeks and waterways beside the Gulf of Guinea. Today, it is known for its busy markets, financial district, music, film, fashion and diverse food culture. The city blends historic neighbourhoods with modern high-rises and a lively waterfront.

Coast, culture and connection

Experience the spirit of Eko

Lagos offers a remarkable mix of experiences. Explore the art galleries and markets, enjoy local dishes such as jollof rice and suya, or take in the coastal scenery around Lekki and the lagoon. Its people, creativity and entrepreneurial spirit make the city distinctive.
